# Question 079: Sharding Strategies When One Primary Is Not Enough

## Context
A single primary saturates CPU and IO despite indexes and pooling. Domain is multi-tenant SaaS. Options floated: Citus-style sharding, application-level shard map, vertical split by bounded contexts.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Compare horizontal sharding keys for multi-tenant SaaS (tenant_id vs other).
2. What cross-shard operations become hard (and how do you avoid them)?
3. Decision framework: when to vertical-split services/databases first vs shard one cluster?
