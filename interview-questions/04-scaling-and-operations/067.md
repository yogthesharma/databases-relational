# Question 067: Connection Storms at Deploy Time

## Context
Every deploy restarts 40 app pods that each open a pool of 20 connections simultaneously. PostgreSQL authenticates a spike and latency collapses for a minute. PgBouncer is not yet deployed.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Diagnose why deploys create connection storms.
2. Design pool settings, startup jitter, and server limits to survive deploys.
3. What health-check antipatterns open extra connections per probe?
