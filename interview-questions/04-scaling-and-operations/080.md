# Question 080: Major Version Upgrades with Minimal Downtime

## Context
You must move from PostgreSQL 14 to 16 on a large DB. Options: in-place `pg_upgrade`, logical replication blue/green, vendor-managed upgrade. Downtime budget is under five minutes.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Compare upgrade strategies and their risk profiles.
2. What incompatibilities and extension issues must you inventory first?
3. Design a cutover rehearsal including connection draining and rollback criteria.
