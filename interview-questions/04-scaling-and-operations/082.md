# Question 082: Synchronous Replication and Cap on Latency

## Context
Finance demands synchronous replication to a cross-AZ standby. Commit latency doubles; occasional stalls when the standby slows. Product asks to keep sync but "make it fast."

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Explain why sync replication adds commit latency.
2. What topologies (quorum sync, multiple standbys) change the failure modes?
3. How do you negotiate RPO vs latency with stakeholders using concrete failure scenarios?
