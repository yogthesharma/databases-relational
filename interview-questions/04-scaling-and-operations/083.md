# Question 083: Archiving Cold Data Without Breaking FKs

## Context
You want to move orders older than two years to cheaper storage while keeping recent OLTP fast. Child tables reference orders. Legal sometimes needs a cold order restored within 24 hours.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Propose an archiving design (partition detach, copy-out, dual DB) and FK handling.
2. How do restores of cold data work without corrupting hot integrity?
3. What query layer changes are needed so apps do not accidentally scan cold data?
