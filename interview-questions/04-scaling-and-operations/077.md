# Question 077: Point-in-Time Recovery Design

## Context
Ransomware deletes critical rows. You have daily base backups and continuous WAL archiving. Leadership asks: "Can we restore to 18:42 UTC yesterday without losing the rest of the estate?"

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Outline PITR steps and how timeline selection works at a conceptual level.
2. What RPO/RTO limits does your backup architecture imply?
3. How do you regularly test restores so this is not theoretical?
