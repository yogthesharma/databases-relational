# Question 069: Logical Replication for Selective Fan-Out

## Context
You need to replicate only the `public.customers` and `public.orders` tables to an analytics Postgres, transforming lightly. Physical replica would include too much sensitive data.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Contrast logical vs physical replication capabilities and limitations.
2. How do you handle sequences, DDL, large updates, and conflict scenarios on the subscriber?
3. What failure modes appear when the publication lag grows for days?
