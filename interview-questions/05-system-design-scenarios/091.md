# Question 091: Feed Fan-Out at Moderate Scale

## Context
A social product with 5M DAU needs home feeds. Celebrities have millions of followers; most users have few. You may use PostgreSQL as system of record with caches.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Compare fan-out-on-write vs fan-out-on-read for this skew.
2. What would you store in Postgres versus Redis/object storage?
3. How do deletes/blocks/privacy changes invalidate feeds correctly?
