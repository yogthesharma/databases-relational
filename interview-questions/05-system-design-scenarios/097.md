# Question 097: Audit-Grade Immutable Ledger

## Context
Fintech regulators require an append-only ledger of balances changes that cannot be silently edited. Analysts still need fast current-balance reads.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Design tables for append-only entries + current balance projection.
2. How do you prevent UPDATE/DELETE on ledger history in PostgreSQL?
3. What reconciliation jobs detect drift between projection and sum of entries?
