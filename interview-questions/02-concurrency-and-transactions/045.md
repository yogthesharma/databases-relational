# Question 045: Read-Only Transactions and Hot Standbys

## Context
Reporting runs `BEGIN READ ONLY` on a hot standby. Occasionally queries are canceled due to recovery conflict when the primary vacuums/deletes old row versions.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Explain recovery conflicts between primary WAL replay and standby queries.
2. What knobs (`max_standby_streaming_delay`, etc.) trade off lag vs query cancellation?
3. How would you architect long analytical reads to avoid hurting replication apply?
