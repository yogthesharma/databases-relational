# Question 043: Predicate Locks vs Row Locks Mentally

## Context
A candidate claims: "If I `SELECT FOR UPDATE` all rows matching a WHERE clause, phantoms cannot occur." Another claims Serializable is the only way to prevent phantoms in PostgreSQL.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Clarify what row locks do and do not guarantee regarding phantoms.
2. How does SSI approximate predicate protection without classic 2PL predicate locks?
3. Give a schema-level technique that prevents a phantom insert from breaking a business rule.
