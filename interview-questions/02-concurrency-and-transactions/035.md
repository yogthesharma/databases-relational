# Question 035: Serializable Snapshot Isolation and Write Skew

## Context
Two doctors schedule the last on-call slot concurrently. Each transaction reads "zero conflicting assignments" and inserts one. Both commit under `READ COMMITTED`. Business rule: at most one on-call doctor.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Name the anomaly and explain why row-level locks on non-existent rows do not prevent it.
2. How does PostgreSQL `SERIALIZABLE` (SSI) aim to prevent this, and what must the application do on conflict?
3. What constraint-based design would enforce the rule without relying solely on isolation level?
