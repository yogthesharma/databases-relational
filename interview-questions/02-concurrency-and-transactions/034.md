# Question 034: Advisory Locks for Application Critical Sections

## Context
A SaaS app must ensure only one "billing run" executes per tenant at a time across many app nodes. Someone proposes a `tenant_billing_locks` table; another proposes `pg_advisory_xact_lock(tenant_id)`.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Contrast session vs transaction advisory locks and their release semantics.
2. When are advisory locks preferable to locking a real row, and when are they dangerous?
3. How would you observe advisory lock waits in production when billing runs stall?
