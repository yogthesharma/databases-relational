# Question 017: Anti-Joins: NOT EXISTS vs NOT IN vs LEFT JOIN

## Context
Find customers with no orders in the last year. Three SQL styles appear in a PR:

- `NOT IN (SELECT customer_id FROM orders ...)`
- `NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM orders o WHERE ...)`
- `LEFT JOIN ... WHERE o.id IS NULL`

`orders.customer_id` is nullable in a legacy schema.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Compare semantic pitfalls of `NOT IN` with NULLs versus `NOT EXISTS`.
2. What plan shapes do you typically expect for each, and which index supports the anti-join well?
3. Which form would you standardize on for this codebase and why?
