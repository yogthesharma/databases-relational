# Question 015: Correlation Statistics and Bad Row Estimates

## Context
A query filters `WHERE city = $1 AND zip = $2`. Independently both are selective enough, but together they are almost functionally dependent. The planner estimates thousands of rows; reality is ~1. Plan chooses a poor join strategy downstream.

## Interviewer Prompt
1. Explain how PostgreSQL's default attribute independence assumption fails here.
2. What tooling (`CREATE STATISTICS`, extended stats) would you use, and what does it change in planning?
3. How would you validate that estimates improved without blindly adding indexes?
